About the OSKAR model

OSKAR is a solution-focused coaching model, developed by Mark McKergow and Paul Z. Jackson ("The Solutions Focus"). Instead of analysing the problem at length, you start from the outcome you want and from what already works.

How to use it with a team:

  • Outcome — help the team describe the desired outcome in concrete, observable terms.
  • Scale — ask for a consensus on the 0–10 scale. The key question isn't "why so low?", but "why not lower?" and "what would move us up a point?".
  • Know-how — surface the resources and what has worked before, so the team leans on its strengths.
  • Affirm & Action — first acknowledge what's already working, then agree on small steps, with an owner and a deadline.
  • Review — at the next meeting: what's better, what helped, what's the next step.

The guidance sits here, at the end, so it doesn't influence the team's answers during the exercise.
